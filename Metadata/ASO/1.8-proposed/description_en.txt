Capture what you see and how you feel, together. SplitCam uses your iPhone’s front and back cameras at the same time to create split-screen photos and videos. Keep the scenery and your reaction in one frame, from travel diaries to everyday moments.

TWO VIEWS, YOUR COMPOSITION
Choose side-by-side, stacked, or picture-in-picture layouts. Adjust the split, move and resize the PiP window, and swap the camera views to tell your story your way.

CREATE ALONGSIDE YOUR MEDIA
Import a photo or video from your library and add your own perspective in Duet mode. Make a reaction, a comparison, or a new take on a favorite moment.

SMALLER FILES WHEN YOU WANT THEM
Choose Space Saver, Standard, or HD video quality. Space Saver uses a smaller video canvas and lower bitrate for lighter files. Settings shows estimated file sizes; actual sizes vary with the scene and recording length. Photo resolution is unaffected.

READY FOR YOUR NEXT SHOT
Choose 9:16, 3:4, 1:1, or 16:9. In 9:16, collapse the frame and quality controls for a clearer preview. Remember your last capture mode, layout, frame, camera swap, and mirror settings, so you can pick up where you left off.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose from a collection of app icons, including Chrome, Terrazzo, Blueprint, and Bloom for Pro.

FREE AND PRO
Photo capture, including PiP photos, is free. Side-by-side and stacked video recording, plus all three video quality options, are also free. Pro unlocks PiP video recording, Duet video recording, and selected app icons.

Monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase are available. Prices and any eligible trial are shown in the app before purchase. Subscriptions renew unless canceled; manage or cancel them in your Apple account’s Subscriptions settings. Restore Purchases is available at the bottom of the Pro page.

Requires iOS 16 or later and an iPhone that supports simultaneous front and back camera capture. Available camera options depend on your device.
